YOLO26

Description

Created by Joseph Redmon in 2016, the original version of YOLO26 was designed to be a fast, and computationally efficient object detection model. Something that at the time had not been seen. Since its development, multiple teams have worked on improving the original concept by Redmon, until we got to the latest version designed by the Ultralytics team, in the form of YOL26.
For our project we used one Ultralytics´s pre-trained YOLO26 models to fine-tune our own version of it, which used our dataset to train it. Resulting in our qualitative examination having both Ultralytics´s model (base YOLO26), and our own "carYOLO26" model (also known as fine-tuned YOLO26). In this page we explain the overarching architecture of YOLO26, which is divided into 3 sections of backbone, neck and head, and provide some examples of how we used YOLO26 to analyse our data.
